Data Deletion Instructions

Removing this app's access

Two ways to do it. The first is immediate and does not involve us.

Last updated 16 September 2026 · Facebook App ID 28453934654262266

What there is to delete. VxD Broadcast Relay stores one thing: a Facebook Page access token for Pages operated by VxD Media. It holds no personal information about viewers, followers, or commenters — so for almost everyone reading this, there is nothing about you to delete. Revoking the token removes everything the application has.

Option 1 — revoke it yourself, immediately

This takes effect the moment you confirm it, and needs nothing from us.

  1. Open Facebook and go to Settings & Privacy → Settings.
  2. Choose Apps and Websites in the left sidebar.
  3. Find VxD Broadcast Relay in the list.
  4. Select Remove, and tick the option to delete posts and videos the app published if you also want those gone.

The application's token stops working at once. It cannot create, publish, or end broadcasts on your Pages afterwards.

Option 2 — ask us to do it

Email contact@vxd.news with the subject line Data deletion request, and tell us which Facebook Page or account the request concerns so we can identify the right token.

We will:

  • Revoke and destroy the stored access token for that Page.
  • Remove any broadcast identifiers still held for it.
  • Confirm to you by email once it is done.

We respond within 30 days, and in practice far sooner.

What deletion does not cover

Live videos already published to a Facebook Page belong to that Page, not to this application. Removing the app's access stops it creating or ending any more, but it does not delete videos already posted. Delete those from the Page itself, or use the "delete posts and videos" option in step 4 above.
